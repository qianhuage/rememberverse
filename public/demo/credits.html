A world made with care

The dog is based on the photograph supplied for this demo. OpenAI image generation prepared a standing reference, and Mint generated its textured 3D mesh. A custom Three.js quadruped deformation rig animates the legs, head and tail in real time. This is a stylized demo interpretation.

Mint generated the detailed floating garden. Three.js provides interactive movement, navigation, lighting, butterflies and offerings. The separate Dream world is a Gaussian-splat environment generated by World Labs.

These assets were prepared before the demo. The credits above describe the tools actually used.
